Shoot square-on to the floor, indoors, no flash, with the bars running up-and-down or across the frame. A dark pit behind the bars reads best. Sun or flash glare on wet bars gives bad readings.

Tap a window to leave it out: galvanized replacement bars, glare, or anything that isn’t tribar. Tap it again to use it.

Saved readings

Width loss = 1 − (bar width ÷ bar pitch) ÷ 0.4686, the width-to-pitch ratio of new tribar measured at C44. It is reported 1:1 as cross-section loss. The band comes from the worst-area width (25th percentile of each window), as in tribar_measure.py.
